# TerraPulse — usefulness and challenge strategy

Reviewed 3 November 2026. This is a candid product assessment, not a prediction of competition results.

## Is TerraPulse useful?

**Yes, as a reproducible surface-change investigation and communication tool.** It makes actual radar observations easier to compare, keeps a physical footprint fixed, distinguishes recent change from persistent difference, shows uncertainty and exports a source-linked dossier. Those are useful intermediate tasks for researchers, environmental teams and educators deciding where to investigate further.

The strongest existing example is the Nepal persistence case: a candidate new-change alert becomes small while the same location remains different from its reference. That distinction is more valuable than an unexplained coloured map. Bangladesh broadens the questions to recurrent radar-dark river signatures and changing wetland responses without claiming that every dark pixel is water.

**What is not established:** user adoption, decisions improved in practice, classification accuracy, validated bank erosion, habitat condition, flood forecasts, operational warning performance or economic benefit. Intended users are a product hypothesis until tested. More dates improve the record; they do not by themselves validate the physical interpretation.

NASA identifies land, water, vegetation and disturbance/recovery as NISAR applications. This supports the relevance of our subject, not a claim that NASA has validated TerraPulse. See [NASA NISAR](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/) and [NASA ecosystem science](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/ecosystem/). NASA's applications program emphasizes engagement with end users and useful information products; our next evidence of impact should therefore come from observed user tasks and feedback. [NASA applications](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/applications/).

## A credible presentation story

**Core sentence:** TerraPulse turns NISAR radar observations into traceable investigations of what changed, what stayed changed and what needs checking next.

1. Open the overview and compare two real Jamuna observations. Identify the data and place immediately.
2. Open Bangladesh: River Memory, Wetland Pulse and Surface change are three questions over the same two accepted study areas. Select a local footprint and follow its actual record.
3. Move to Nepal. Demonstrate why a small new-change alert is different from recovery. Show the reference and recent-change traces, then the longer observation record.
4. Inspect coherence as an additional measurement and present competing explanations. Show where corroboration is missing rather than implying causal certainty.
5. Export an evidence dossier. Explain the next practical action: independent review or a targeted field visit.

The memorable moment should come from a scientific distinction the audience understands, supported by actual imagery and clear interaction. Decorative animation is secondary. The interface now uses a restrained dark landscape theme, a real radar comparison and country-specific routes; data maps retain their scientific display scales.

## Why these applications matter, and what their limits are

- **River Memory:** supports screening of recurring and changing low-return patterns. A user can choose locations for closer inspection. A validated bank-migration measurement needs registered independent channel/bank boundaries and an uncertainty model.
- **Wetland Pulse:** supports comparison of temporal scattering patterns and selection of investigation footprints. Habitat quality, vegetation species and actual inundation duration require suitable independent evidence.
- **Bangladesh Surface change:** offers a common footprint-analysis workflow across Jamuna and Tanguar. It is not another independently acquired Bangladesh study region.
- **Nepal Surface investigations:** supports persistence analysis around a tracked disturbance and a reference-aligned impact corridor. Spatial coincidence with a preliminary map does not prove cause or accuracy.
- **Environmental context:** helps examine possible explanations using terrain, optical/thermal imagery and rainfall assumptions. A susceptibility index remains uncalibrated and separate from measured radar change.

## Evidence of usefulness to collect before submission

Recruit a small convenience sample representing the intended users, and identify it honestly as exploratory research. Suggested target: five participants, including at least one person with relevant Earth-observation or environmental experience. No contacts have been messaged by the assistant.

Ask each person to: (1) find the Bangladesh wetland tool, (2) choose and replay a real visit, (3) inspect one footprint, (4) explain reference difference versus recent change, (5) identify one uncertainty, and (6) export a dossier. Record task completion, navigation errors, time, interpretation errors and a verbatim statement of the next action they would take. Suggested design target: at least four of five complete the core path unaided, with no participant interpreting a missing visit as zero or a susceptibility score as flood probability. These are proposed acceptance targets, not achieved results.

For scientific corroboration, predefine sample footprints before examining candidate outputs. Use acquisition-matched, quality-screened optical or field references where available. Report selection limitations and disagreement. Do not optimize thresholds and report validation on the same samples.

## Submission and reported AI review

No AI screening model, weights or pass threshold has been provided. There is no defensible guarantee of winning or effortlessly passing automated review. Prepare a readable, machine-accessible submission with the same evidence a human judge needs:

- A concise problem, intended user, workflow, NASA datasets and geographic scope.
- Working run instructions, data-access/license information and accessible repository/video links.
- Plain-language explanations beside figures; text equivalents for important conclusions.
- A claim-to-evidence trail, scientific limitations and AI-use disclosure.
- Actual team contributions and user/community feedback, without invented endorsements or keyword stuffing.

The local guide asks for presentation videos and AI disclosure. Final lengths, deadlines, public access and treatment of work completed before the event need confirmation from the applicable organizer. This project already contains work predating the upcoming event; preserve that history and disclose it where required. Do not imply eligibility has been certified.
