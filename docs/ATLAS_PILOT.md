# TerraPulse workflow pilot · frozen protocol v1

**Status: waiting for participants.** There are zero completed sessions. Recruit three consenting people who might review Earth observations, such as a river or wetland practitioner, a geospatial analyst or a disaster researcher. One independent reviewer must reopen a dossier created by someone else. A reviewer may be one of the three participants if the handoff is independent.

Use the audited saved edition and record its release ID. Use IDs such as P01 instead of names. Record role category, device, elapsed time, outcome, hints and whether the proposed next check changed. Do not collect names, contact details, agency identifiers, personal locations or recordings in the results. Ask each participant whether they agree to anonymous task notes, and stop if they decline. Allow five minutes per task and do not explain the intended interpretation before the task.

Score each task as **unassisted**, **assisted**, **incomplete** or **stopped**. A neutral repeat of the prompt is allowed. Naming a control, interpreting a number or providing the answer counts as assistance. Log wrong interpretations even if the final answer is corrected. At the end ask what evidence the participant would request next and what changed their mind.

Rank inability to open, verify or distinguish the two comparisons as blocking. Rank an avoidable error in alternative explanations or field checks as major. Fix blocking issues and repeat the affected task with the new release ID, or remove the affected scope. Report counts and observed outcomes with sample size, without inferring scientific accuracy or broader impact.
