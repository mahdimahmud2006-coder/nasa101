# TerraPulse independent validation protocol · v1

Frozen design for future validation. Current HLS comparisons are descriptive and are excluded from the holdout. Any amendment gets a new version before held-out labels are opened. No thresholds were tuned for this release.

| Proposed claim | Assessment unit | Independent reference | Release threshold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tanguar open water | 30 m cell inside a sampled site-date block | Same-day field water observations, or clear same-day aerial/optical interpretation by two blinded reviewers | Lower 95% bounds for both precision and recall at least 0.80 |
| Jamuna signed bank movement | Predeclared orthogonal transect and pair of dates | Independent co-registered bank survey or delineation on both dates, each within 24 hours and with comparable water level or a documented level correction | Lower 95% bound for direction agreement at least 0.80 and upper 95% bound for median absolute error at most one 20 m GCOV posting |
| Nepal event damage proxy | Site-date block within a dated, independently surveyed event | Independently mapped damaged and undamaged sites within the event window and before substantial recovery, with documented inclusion and location error | Lower 95% bounds for precision and recall at least 0.80 and upper 95% bound for false-positive rate at most 0.10 |

Habitat condition, flooded vegetation, exposure, duration and displacement need separate designs. The preceding targets are prospective project criteria. They are not external standards or demonstrated accuracy.

1. Before viewing labels, delineate site blocks and their exclusion buffers at least as wide as the largest analysis window or empirically supported spatial dependence range from development data. Assign entire connected blocks to development or holdout. Bind all overlapping windows and all dates at a site to one split. Partition scene/granule IDs across splits too, including both endpoints of any pair. If these constraints leave too few blocks, acquire new sites and scenes. Keep a split manifest with source checksums, block geometry and time bounds.
2. Predefine reference units before reading values. For class area, use a probability sample with known inclusion probabilities and stratify by mapped class and geography. Preserve block clustering, count uncovered strata, and predeclare sample size using a precision calculation. A holdout needs at least 10 independent blocks and 30 assessable units in each target and comparison class, and a design capable of meeting the interval thresholds. Small subgroups are reported as insufficient.
3. Record case, footprint or transect, observation and source scene IDs, timestamps and signed offsets, block and split, geometry, inclusion probability, spatial registration error, QA, two independent annotations and adjudication. Exclude cloud, shadow, ambiguous water/vegetation, unresolved crossings, missing endpoints, out-of-window labels and insufficient registration. Count each reason and retain uncertain labels in the ledger. Reviewers stay blind to radar class, scores and split where practical.
4. Tune rules only on development. Freeze code, thresholds and comparison method before opening holdout labels. Report weighted confusion, precision, recall and false-positive rate, or signed bias, median absolute error and direction agreement, plus support, failures and subgroup breakdown. Use design-based intervals or a predeclared cluster bootstrap that respects site and scene dependence. If interval estimates are unstable, publish support and withhold the claim.
5. Report projected and WGS84 boundary area separately from valid support and raw class area. Publish area adjustment and its interval only with a validated class, known inclusion weights, defensible coverage and independent reproduction of the estimator. Exposure additionally needs a validated and dated exposure layer. Dated optical overlap remains a separate comparison with its own support and date offset.

[Olofsson et al., probability sampling and area estimation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2014.02.015).
