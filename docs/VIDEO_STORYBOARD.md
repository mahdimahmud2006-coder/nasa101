## Recording discipline

- Record the actual local application. No synthetic SAR maps, filled cloud gaps, interpolated visits or staged fake processing animations.
- Keep source dates, footprint scope, provisional/uncalibrated status and limitations visible in close-ups. Use readable text and captions; do not rely on music/color alone.
- Say “observed visits” when describing persistence. Never equate the original nominal pair areas with the centered-score tracked-zone area.
- Show the authentic weak Jamuna overlap as well as the stronger Tanguar comparison; no selective claim of validated water detection.
- The new keyboard/navigation implementation is not a claim of complete WCAG conformance. Physical disconnected and real-browser QA remain pending until actual rehearsal evidence exists.
- The build is a local submission candidate. Public hosting, video upload, judge access and competition submission are separate actions and have not been performed by this work.

Primary radar background for the optional opening: [NASA Earthdata SAR introduction](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/earth-observation-data-basics/sar). Verify any added scientific narration against primary sources and add its ledger entry before recording.
