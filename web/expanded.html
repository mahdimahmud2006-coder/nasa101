What coherence adds

Coherence measures similarity of the radar phase between two acquisitions. A low value can accompany changing surfaces, vegetation, water, imaging geometry or noise. It does not identify a cause.

Pair summaries describe the entire native crop, not the selected intensity footprint. The 20 m and 80 m estimates use different support.
