      Model and limits

      The teaching model mixes a soil or water surface with canopy volume scattering and a water–stem double bounce. The canopy control is an assumed proxy. It does not measure biomass or DpRVIc. Similar radar returns can come from different surfaces.

      Model and limits

      Across-bank radar-dark recurrence uses observed HH at or below the screening cutoff. The scenario scales the observed edge excursion by its recurrence, the multiplier and the assumed number of cycles. It has no measured discharge relationship. The corridor is a local sensitivity exercise; it does not establish erosion, a future water zone, or the location of real embankments.

      Model and limits

      Parallel rays are traced across a buffered NASADEM grid. A local range slope flags layover where it reverses near-to-far ordering. Wavelength changes the phase sensitivity example; it does not move geometric shadows. The C-band example is simulated. Observed L-band coherence also depends on temporal change, baseline, geometry and noise.

      Field Guide · read this observation

      HH and HV are radar polarization channels. A dB difference compares mean backscatter on the same footprint. The previous visit shows recent change. The reference shows the difference from a fixed starting observation. The table labels unavailable values explicitly.

      Coherence measures how similar the radar signal is across a recorded pair. Thermal and optical scenes keep their own acquisition dates. Use dated independent evidence to investigate the cause of a radar change.

      Choose Find comparison data to look up NASA water maps for Bangladesh or vegetation disturbance maps for Nepal near the radar dates. Review the time offset and map coverage before drawing a conclusion.
